# Code of conduct

TwinLine is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly one. Everyone
who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the
project is represented) is expected to follow this code. It is based on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Be respectful when you disagree; criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the
contact on [Yusuf Enes Kütük's GitHub profile](https://github.com/yusuffeness).
Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who
does so, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.
